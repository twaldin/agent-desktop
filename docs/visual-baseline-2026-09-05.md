Offsets are zero-based Unicode character positions in the pinned files, not generated source-map claims. `I-CSS` means `app-initial-5b0a474bff5e.css`; `P-CSS` means `app-primary-7fe7c6486695.css`; these are preserved under `.reference/codex-26.901.41600/visual-sources/webview/assets/`.
